Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

YS DATA ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what information the YS DATA app collects and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account details you give us: full name, phone number and email address.
  • Your wallet balance and transaction history (purchases, wallet funding, recipient phone numbers, amounts and status).
  • A transaction PIN, which we store only in a protected (hashed) form. We cannot read it.
  • If you turn on fingerprint unlock, your phone checks your fingerprint on the device. We do not receive or store your fingerprint.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and process your purchases.
  • To deliver the data or airtime you ask for to the phone number you enter.
  • To show your history, send receipts, prevent fraud and give you support.

Payments

Wallet funding is processed by Paystack. We do not see or store your full card details. Paystack handles them under its own privacy policy.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share only what is needed with the services that run the app: our cloud database and authentication provider (Google Firebase), our payment processor (Paystack), and the telecom service providers that fulfil your data and airtime orders (they receive the recipient number and the order details).

Your choices and account deletion

You can update your name and phone number in the app. You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account. Deleting your account removes your profile, PIN and wallet balance permanently. Records of past transactions may be kept where needed for accounting and fraud prevention.

Security

We use encrypted connections and access rules so that each user can only reach their own data. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your PIN private.

Children

YS DATA is not intended for children under 13.

The service

YS DATA is a platform for buying mobile data and airtime. We resell services that are delivered by telecom networks and their authorised partners. Delivery depends on those networks being available.

  • You must give correct information and keep your password and transaction PIN private.
  • You are responsible for activity done with your PIN.

Wallet and payments

  • You fund your wallet through Paystack. Funds are added after the payment is confirmed.
  • Purchases are paid from your wallet balance at the price shown in the app before you confirm.
  • Prices can change. The price you confirm is the price you pay.

Orders and refunds

  • Please check the phone number and network carefully. Data or airtime sent to a wrong number cannot be recovered.

  • Wallet balance is for buying services in the app.
  • Some data plans may not work on a number that is owing the network or is not eligible for that plan type.
